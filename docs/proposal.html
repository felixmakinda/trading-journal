The key discovery

Pepperstone only lets you trade on TradingView through a Razor account on cTrader. That means your trades actually live inside cTrader, and cTrader has an official, free API. So the journal can pull your trades in automatically with read-only access. No CSV uploads, no typing entries by hand.

2. How a normal trading day would flow

The journal fits around your routine instead of adding work to it.

sequenceDiagram autonumber actor You participant J as Journal participant TV as TradingView participant CT as cTrader (Pepperstone) You->>J: Quick plan + checklist You->>TV: Place the trade TV->>CT: Order executed J->>CT: Fetch new deals (every 15 min) CT-->>J: Entry, exit, lots, costs J->>J: Match to plan, compute P&L J-->>You: Alerts (rule break, revenge, limit) You->>J: Notes, emotions, chart link J-->>You: Weekly review report
StepWhat happens
1Before the trade you jot a quick plan: setup, entry, stop, target, and tick your checklist. Optional.
2-3You trade on TradingView as usual. The order executes on your Pepperstone Razor account in cTrader.
4-5Every 15 minutes the journal fetches new deals (read-only), including commission and swap.
6-7It matches the trade to your plan, computes P&L in $, pips and R, and flags rule breaks, revenge trades or a nearby daily loss limit.
8-9You add emotions, notes and a TradingView snapshot link. At the end of the week you get a review report (with an AI summary later).

What "matching to your plan" gives you

  • A rule score per trade: did you follow your checklist, respect your stop, size correctly?
  • A clear split between good trades that lost (followed the plan) and bad trades that won (broke the rules but got lucky). This is where most improvement comes from.
  • Plans are optional. If you skip one, the trade still syncs and shows up as "unplanned".

3. What the journal would do

Grouped by priority. Phase 1-3 is the first usable version. Later comes after you have used it for a while.

Core journal Phase 1-2

  • Trade list with filters (pair, session, setup, tag, date)
  • Calendar of daily P&L
  • Dashboard: win rate, average win and loss, profit factor, expectancy, drawdown
  • Notes, tags, screenshots, TradingView snapshot links shown as images
  • Export everything to CSV or JSON at any time

Automatic sync Phase 3

  • Connect your cTrader ID once, read-only
  • New trades appear within about 15 minutes
  • Commission and swap included, so P&L matches Pepperstone
  • Backup: import Pepperstone statements or TradingView CSV

Forex-specific Phase 4

  • Results in dollars, pips and R (multiples of what you risked)
  • Session stats: Asia, London, New York, shown in Nairobi time
  • Best and worst pairs, days and hours
  • Prop-firm tracker: daily drawdown and max loss with warnings (if you trade funded accounts)

Behaviour and psychology Phase 4

  • Revenge trade alert: bigger lot soon after a loss
  • Overtrading and losing-streak (tilt) detection
  • Mood, sleep and emotion tags compared with results
  • Your own rules, tracked and scored

AI coach Later

  • Weekly review written from your real numbers
  • "Ask my journal": e.g. Why do I lose on Fridays?
  • Choose free, paid, or fully private AI (see section 5)

Phone friendly Later

  • Install on your phone like an app (no app store needed)
  • Log a note or emotion in about 10 seconds

5. AI coach and keeping your data safe

How the AI would work (later phase)

  • The AI never does the maths. The journal calculates every number, and the AI only explains it. That stops it from inventing figures.
  • It sees summaries, not your account. No login details, balances or account numbers are sent.
  • You can switch AI provider in settings, with a monthly spending cap. Free tiers have daily limits that change often, so the journal keeps working when a limit is hit.

Security, in plain terms

  • Read-only broker access. The journal can see trades but can never place, change or close one, and can never withdraw.
  • The cTrader connection key is encrypted and never sent to your browser.
  • Login with 2-step verification. Every row of data is locked to your account at database level.
  • Automatic daily backups, plus a full export button.
  • Every code change is automatically tested and scanned for security problems before it goes live.
Note: the journal records and analyses. It does not give trade signals or place trades. The AI's suggestions are reflections on your own history, not financial advice.
